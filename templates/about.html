About Emotion-Aware Yoga AI

Emotion-Aware Yoga AI is an intelligent wellness platform that analyzes your journal entries using Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing to understand your emotional well-being and recommend personalized yoga practices with clear explanations.

Our Mission

Mental health is unique for every individual. Traditional yoga applications often provide generic recommendations without considering emotions or physical symptoms. Our mission is to bridge the gap between Artificial Intelligence and holistic wellness by providing personalized, explainable, and safe yoga recommendations tailored to each user's emotional condition.
